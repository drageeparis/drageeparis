The Workshop

A workshop devoted to creation, personalisation and the art of detail. Welcome to the world of Dragée Paris.

The art of detail

Dragée Paris was born of a passion for creation, personalisation and the world of dragées. From our workshop in Île-de-France, we design containers and compositions intended to give every event its own unique identity.

We carefully select the dragées, materials, colours, ribbons, labels and finishes. Each creation is then assembled and personalised with care, according to your wishes and the spirit of your celebration.

Weddings, christenings, births, birthdays and receptions: every year, our creations accompany many events in Île-de-France and throughout France.

What guides us

Craftsmanship, selection, bespoke design: three commitments behind every one of our creations.

Craftsmanship

Every creation is imagined and assembled by hand in our Île-de-France workshop. We carefully personalise each container to create unique pieces, designed to elevate your events.

Selection

We carefully select quality dragées, elegant materials and refined finishes. Every element is chosen to create a harmonious whole, worthy of your most special occasions.

Bespoke

Colours, containers, ribbons, labels and finishes: each creation is personalised to suit your event and your wishes, for a unique ensemble that truly reflects you.

Where to find us

Workshop in Île-de-France, delivery throughout France and Europe.

Our workshop is located in Île-de-France. We mainly take orders remotely, by phone, by email or through our form, and deliver throughout France and Europe.

Workshop appointments can be arranged on request for large orders or for clients who would like to see the creations in person.
