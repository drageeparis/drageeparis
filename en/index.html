FAQ

Lead times · Personalisation · Delivery

5 to 10 days for dragées sold by weight, 4 to 6 weeks for personalised creations.
For a bespoke creation, orders start at 20 boxes; dragées sold by weight start from 250 g. Whether you are preparing a few dozen boxes or several hundred for a wedding, a christening or a corporate event, we are with you every step of the way.
Following your quote request, we help you choose the colours, finishes, accessories and personalisation. Before any preparation begins, we produce a visual of your creation for your approval, so that you can sign off every detail with complete peace of mind.
We deliver your creations throughout France and Europe, in careful, reinforced packaging. For dragées sold by weight, delivery to a Mondial Relay pick-up point is free up to 1 kg, then charged at €12 per additional kilo or part thereof. For creations, shipping costs are set out in your quote. Collection from our Athis-Mons workshop is available by appointment.
