The art of the birth dragée

Offering dragées to announce a birth is a tradition handed down from generation to generation. Our birth dragées are matched to your decoration, right down to the label with the baby's name; for a baby shower or a gender reveal, they come in pink, blue or a mixed version.

The Avola almond from Sicily is the dragée of excellence; the traditional almond, the one with the authentic taste; chocolate, with its melting centre, the one for food lovers.

For a personalised creation, we recommend contacting us four to six weeks in advance. Each piece is assembled by hand in our Athis-Mons workshop, then delivered throughout France and Europe.
