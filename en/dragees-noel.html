The dragée, a festive guest

Placed beside each plate on Christmas Eve, tucked into a gift or offered to your loved ones, the dragée carries the sweetness of the festive season. Our Christmas collection pairs burgundy, ivory, gold and fir green with natural finishes: dried flowers, pine cones, tartan ribbon and jute twine.

Each creation is assembled by hand in our Athis-Mons workshop, with a label personalised with your name or message. For delivery before Christmas, order as early as possible: we confirm the date as soon as we receive your request.
