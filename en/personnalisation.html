Personalisation

Every Dragée Paris creation is unique. We tailor colours, flavours, packaging and inscriptions to your event, from the first idea to delivery.

A touch of
delicacy

A dragée is far more than a confection: it is a gesture, a token of attention, an act of care. At Dragée Paris, every dragée is chosen for its quality, the softness of its coating and the precision of its shade.

We choose our finishes the way a jeweller chooses metals: matt for understated elegance, satin for a soft sheen, pearlescent for gentle light, subtly metallic for ultimate refinement.

The art of
celebration

It all began with a simple conviction: life's most beautiful moments deserve beautiful gifts. Gifts that are kept, photographed and offered with emotion to every guest.

Our creations pay tribute to the generosity of great French celebrations, blending the tradition of the dragée with complete personalisation and modern design. Because your event is unique, so is your dragée.

The journey

3 steps to order with peace of mind, from the first idea to delivery.

01

Describe your project

Fill in our form in just a few minutes: occasion, desired products, estimated quantities. We will get back to you within 48 hours with a tailored proposal.
