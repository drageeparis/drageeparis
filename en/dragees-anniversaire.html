The art of the birthday dragée

Unicorn, panda, ice floe or ocean world: each creation tells a story. Our birthday dragées are matched to the party theme, right down to the label with the child's name.

The Avola almond from Sicily is the dragée of excellence; the traditional almond, the one with the authentic taste; chocolate, with its melting centre, the one for food lovers.

We recommend contacting us four to six weeks before the birthday. Each piece is assembled by hand in our Athis-Mons workshop, then delivered throughout France and Europe.
