The art of the christening dragée

Tradition has it that each guest receives five dragées, for five wishes: health, happiness, prosperity, fertility and long life. Our christening dragées are matched to your decoration, right down to the label with the child's name and the date of the ceremony.

The Avola almond from Sicily is the dragée of excellence; the traditional almond, the one with the authentic taste; chocolate, with its melting centre, the one for food lovers.

We recommend contacting us four to six weeks before the christening. Each piece is assembled by hand in our Athis-Mons workshop, then delivered throughout France and Europe.
